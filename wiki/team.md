## Our Story

We are a small, focused team of high school students from **Shanghai Hongwen School Pudong** with a shared obsession: turning industrial waste streams into something useful. Our team has been working together on FTC robotics for two years, and the iGEM 2026 season is our first time crossing over into synthetic biology.

The same engineering habits we built in FTC - rapid iteration, modular design, public code - now drive our biology work.

## What Makes Our Team Different

1. **We bring engineering culture from FTC into biology.** Most iGEM teams are biology-first. We are engineering-first: we design the hardware *first*, then ask the wet-lab to make the cells fit it. This is unusual, and it's why we ship faster.
2. **All our code is open-source.** Every script, every CAD file, every model lives on GitHub from day 1. No proprietary lock-in.
3. **Rapid iteration under real constraints.** Our 16-hour V1 -> V2 hardware iteration shows the team's core value: when something works, ship it; when it doesn't, throw it away and try again.
4. **Cross-school collaboration.** The same ivymaker mentor who supports our team also supports teams at 5+ other schools. We see this as a national iGEM network, not a single-team project.
